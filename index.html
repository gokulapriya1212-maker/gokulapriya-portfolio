MedRoute AI

An NLP-Based Medical Specialist and Nearby Hospital Recommendation System

FEATURED
PROJECT

MedRoute AI is a healthcare navigation concept designed to help users move from a natural-language symptom description to relevant medical specialty guidance and nearby hospital options.

NLP + LOCATION-BASED DISCOVERY

MedRoute AI — college project presentation

01

Problem statement

People may find it difficult to identify an appropriate medical specialty from their symptom descriptions and locate nearby hospitals that offer the required specialty.

02

Objective

Interpret symptom descriptions in natural language, identify relevant medical specialties and simplify location-based discovery of nearby hospitals.

03

Proposed workflow

Symptom and location input → NLP preprocessing → specialty classification → nearby hospital search → recommendation results.

04

Expected outcome

A simpler navigation experience that connects symptom understanding with specialty guidance and location-based hospital discovery.
